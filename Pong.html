Training

ε falls from 1 to 0 over the first 60% of the steps. The learner holds the right paddle; each training episode is one rally. The state is whether the ball is coming, how far it is, where it is relative to the paddle, its vertical speed and its height band, cut into bins.

Dashed lines mark the distance and height bins the machine sees. The strip beside its paddle slices its Q-table for the ball as it is now: each cell is a place the paddle's centre could be, split into Q(up), Q(stay) and Q(down), tinted green for positive and red for negative. The chalk mark is the move it makes there. Point at a cell to read its values.

ε = 0.00

Chance, on every tick, that the machine ignores its Q-table and moves at random. At 0, a well-trained paddle seldom misses, so aim for its corners with steep returns.

Game speed

The ball leaves the paddle at an angle set by where it hits: the centre sends it straight, the tips send it up to 62° off. Every return makes it a little faster.

↑ ↓ or W S move your paddle, as do the buttons under the board or a drag on it. Space serves; P pauses.
