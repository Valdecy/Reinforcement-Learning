Training

ε falls from 1 to 0 over the first 60% of the steps. Every training flight gets a fresh random course. The state is the distance to the next pipe, the height of its gap relative to the bird, and the bird's vertical speed, cut into bins.

The bins in front of the machine's next pipe, at its current speed. The top half of each cell is tinted by Q(flap), the bottom half by Q(glide): green for positive, red for negative. The chalk mark is the move the bird makes there: a chevron to flap, a dash to glide. Point at a cell to read its values.

ε = 0.00

Chance, on every tick, that the machine ignores its Q-table and flaps or glides at random. At 0, a well-trained bird rarely crashes, so the best you can usually do is a draw.

Game speed

Both birds fly the same pipes with the same physics, side by side. The first to hit a pipe, the ground or the ceiling loses. Crossing the finish line together, or crashing on the same tick, is a draw.

Space, ↑ or W flaps; so does a click or tap on the board. P pauses.
