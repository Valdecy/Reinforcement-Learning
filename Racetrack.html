Training

ε falls from 1 to 0 over the first 60% of the steps; switching method resets α to the value that suits it here. The state is exact: the square and the velocity (each component 0–4), in all. As in the book, a crash sends the car back to a random start square at rest, and the episode goes on.

Every square of the track at the machine's current velocity. The green is how soon it expects to finish from there; brighter is sooner, and squares it has never tried at that velocity stay blank. The chalk mark is the acceleration it would choose. Point at a square to read all nine values.

ε = 0.00

Chance, on every turn, that the machine ignores its Q-table and accelerates at random. At 0 it drives the line it learned; to beat it, find a faster one.

Both cars move at the same time each turn. Your next square is your current velocity plus the acceleration you choose, which the board marks with nine dots; a red cross means that move crashes. Crossing the line on the same turn, or nobody finishing within 100 turns, is a draw.

Keys: Q W E / A S D / Z X C or the number pad pick the nine accelerations; the arrows pick the four straight ones and Space coasts.
